# Changelog

## 1.0.0-rc1 (2026-11-26)

The first release candidate of **Starjux**, a top-down 2D space construction and automation sandbox.

### Build anything from square parts
- One blueprint editor for ships, stations, factories, farms, mines and swarms, with grids up to 101 × 101, mirroring, undo and redo, and reusable modules.
- Over 100 parts. The workhorse parts come in Mk I to Mk IV.
- Shipyards build, refit, repair, dismantle and pack craft into construction kits that you deploy in orbit or on a surface.

### Fly and work in a living star system
- Fly your own ship with real thrust, local gravity, a cruise drive, docking, landing and anchoring.
- Mine bodies that lose mass as you dig. You can take a whole body apart, or move it to a new orbit with mass drivers.
- Warp gates, further star systems, star types, exotic ore and a galaxy map.
- Hazards: solar flares, meteor showers and comets.

### Automate it
- Mining, freight, survey, salvage, guard, escort and patrol jobs. Each one always says why it is waiting.
- A programmable job builder, station requests, swarms of identical miners, and a production planner.
- A thousand craft run at full speed. Craft far from you are simulated cheaply.

### Keep it running
- Power from solar panels to antimatter, fuel chains, batteries and power priorities.
- Crew, robots, food, water and life support.
- Research, milestones and megastructures built in stages.
- Native life and animals, civilizations with races and laws, trade, diplomacy, contracts, raids and combat with guns, lasers, missiles, shields and armour.
- Mark your own defunct craft as wrecks, and salvagers take them apart.

### Play your way
- Pick a race or create your own, choose your world options, name your game and pick its seed.
- Save slots and autosave.
- Rebindable keys and controller menus.
- Interface scale, captions, sound and music.

### Notes
- The game was called "Space Automation Sandbox" before it had its name. On first start, Starjux copies your saves, designs and settings from that older folder. The old folder is left as it was.
